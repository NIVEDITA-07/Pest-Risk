=== ABSTRACT (53-59) ===
[53] Abstract

[54] In today's connected digital era, elderly seniors living independently face heightened vulnerabilities from sudden medical incapacitation, unobserved falls, or acute distress. In these life-threatening situations, seniors are physically incapable of answering incoming telephone calls or dialing emergency services. Although family members, caregivers, and medical practitioners make repeated urgent calls, conventional mobile telephony platforms treat these high-stakes calls identically to marketing spam or routine chatter, offering zero ambient intelligence or automated distress escalation. Conversely, black-box Machine Learning (ML) and Large Language Model (LLM) approaches suffer from severe drawbacks in safety-critical eldercare, including non-deterministic hallucinations, inference latency, battery drain, lack of legal auditability, and invasive eavesdropping that violates HIPAA and GDPR privacy.

[55] To resolve this challenge, this project proposes SafeCall AI: Connected Elderly-Care Telephony & Urgency Platform, an intelligent, dual-tier connected healthcare and ambient assisted living (AAL) system engineered specifically for elderly individuals living independently. Built strictly with deterministic Java and Android OS native facilities, SafeCall AI intercepts telephony telemetry in real time, computes contextual call urgency scores (0–100), detects severe non-response anomalies, and dispatches multi-channel caregiver alerts—with zero cloud dependency for AI inference, zero ML hallucinations, and zero audio eavesdropping.

[56] The system operates through an asynchronous telephony interception Finite State Machine (FSM) utilizing Android OS native facilities. Incoming calls are evaluated through two complementary, deterministic Java engines: a 4-Factor Weighted Rule Engine that computes instantaneous call urgency (0–100) across caller priority, call burst frequency, late-night timing, and historical answer rates; and a 5-Component Non-Response Policy Engine that monitors sequential unanswered bursts across rolling windows. To eliminate false alarms and caregiver alert fatigue, SafeCall AI enforces a strict 3-Gate Anomaly Pipeline comprising a frequency threshold (≥4 missed calls), a 60-second grace cancellation period, and a configurable severity check.

[57] SafeCall AI incorporates dual-tier emergency dispatch, combining real-time cloud push alerts via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) through a companion Node.js backend with direct, offline carrier-grade cellular SMS to primary and nearby family members. An embedded SQLite transactional queue ensures zero data loss during network dead zones, automatically synchronizing queued alerts upon reconnection. Companion interfaces—including an embedded React 18 healthcare UI on the senior's handset and a dedicated React 18 Caregiver Web Dashboard with live urgency visualizers, benchmark scenario testing, and telemetry gauges—provide seamless real-time monitoring and parameter testing.

[58] Overall, SafeCall AI serves as a transparent, auditable, and life-saving digital guardian, bridging mobile systems engineering, deterministic algorithmic intelligence, modern React component design, and ambient assisted living to safeguard elderly independence and provide peace of mind to families.

[84] 1.1  GENERAL

[85] In the modern era of rapid technological advancement and changing social demographics, the global population is experiencing a profound transition toward aging societies. A significant proportion of senior citizens now live independently in their own homes, valuing autonomy and familiar environments. However, this independent lifestyle introduces grave personal safety vulnerabilities. Seniors frequently experience unobserved slip-and-fall accidents, sudden cardiovascular incidents, strokes, or acute diabetic episodes that render them physically incapacitated and unable to move, answer incoming calls, or reach for help.

[86] When such traumatic events occur, concerned family members, caregivers, and medical practitioners invariably attempt repeated phone calls. On conventional mobile devices, however, standard telephony subsystems treat these life-or-death redials identically to routine telemarketing spam or casual missed calls. Standard operating systems provide zero automated escalation, zero contextual analysis, and zero emergency outreach when multiple critical incoming calls go unanswered.

[87] While modern software solutions have attempted to apply Machine Learning (ML) and Large Language Models (LLMs) to call evaluation, these black-box paradigms present unacceptable hazards in safety-critical eldercare. Machine learning models suffer from non-deterministic hallucinations under edge conditions, introduce high inference latency, drain scarce handset battery reserves, and require continuous cloud connectivity. More critically, audio-based AI eavesdropping violates stringent healthcare privacy regulations such as HIPAA and GDPR, while offering no legally verifiable or mathematically auditable justification for emergency dispatches.

[88] To resolve this fundamental dilemma, the SafeCall AI project was conceived as an intelligent, dual-tier connected healthcare and ambient assisted living (AAL) system. Operating strictly through deterministic Java algorithms, native Android telephony telemetry, modern React 18 user interfaces, and a dedicated Node.js cloud caregiver backend, SafeCall AI evaluates incoming call metadata in real time, computes urgency scores (0–100), detects severe non-response anomalies, and dispatches multi-channel caregiver alerts—with zero cloud dependency for inference, zero ML hallucinations, and zero audio eavesdropping.

[89] 1.2  OBJECT-ORIENTED PROGRAMMING (OOP)

[90] Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) is a foundational software engineering paradigm that structures complex software systems around discrete objects—instances of classes that encapsulate state, behavior, and relationships. Unlike procedural programming, which organizes logic as sequential subroutine instructions, OOP models real-world operational domains and their collaborative interactions, directly promoting reusability, testability, and enterprise maintainability.

[91] The four foundational pillars of OOP form the architectural backbone of SafeCall AI:

[92] •  Encapsulation: Bundling of critical telemetry attributes (timestamps, contact priorities, historical answer rates) within immutable or controlled data models (e.g., CallContext, CallerProfile, CallRecord, PendingSequence), shielding internal states from arbitrary external corruption.

[93] •  Inheritance: Deriving specialized components from extensible framework abstractions, such as DatabaseHelper extending Android's SQLiteOpenHelper, and SafeCallPhoneStateReceiver extending BroadcastReceiver, promoting code reuse and modular system behavior.

[94] •  Abstraction: Concealing internal algorithmic complexity behind clean, well-defined service interfaces (e.g., WeightedRuleEngine.evaluateCall(), FamilyAlertManager.dispatchAlert()), enabling client activities and web dashboards to execute domain logic without coupling to internal rule evaluation mechanics.

[95] •  Polymorphism: Enabling interchangeable treatment of polymorphic call outcomes (ANSWERED, MISSED, DECLINED, OUTGOING_CALLBACK) and multi-channel alert delivery adapters (Cloud HTTP FCM push versus direct cellular SMS), allowing runtime substitution based on connectivity states.

[96] In SafeCall AI, these OOP principles are systematically enforced across both handset-side Android services and the component-oriented React frontend architecture, creating a fully decoupled, enterprise-grade system capable of rapid extension and rigorous verification.

[99] 1.3  PROJECT OVERVIEW

[100] SafeCall AI is an end-to-end, multi-tier software ecosystem that unites on-device native Android Java intelligence with a modern React 18 user interface and a cloud-connected caregiver dashboard. The project bridges the divide between passive mobile operating systems and intelligent healthcare monitoring by transforming raw telephony events into actionable clinical indicators without violating patient privacy.

[101] At the foundation of the handset tier is the SafeCallPhoneStateReceiver, which implements an explicit Finite State Machine (FSM) to track call lifecycle transitions (IDLE → RINGING → OFFHOOK → IDLE) with zero UI thread contention. Intercepted events are immediately offloaded to a background thread pool (CallEventProcessor.EXECUTOR), ensuring that telephony operations complete in under 5 milliseconds and eliminating Application Not Responding (ANR) risks.

[102] The project incorporates two complementary mathematical evaluation engines:

[103] •  The 4-Factor Weighted Rule Engine (WeightedRuleEngine): Evaluates incoming calls instantaneously across four additive gates: Caller Identity Priority (0–25 pts), Call Frequency within a 15-minute rolling window (0–35 pts), Timing Context such as late-night hours (0–10 pts), and Historical Anomaly Deviation (0–30 pts). The total score is strictly bounded to [0, 100], classifying calls into LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH urgency.

[104] •  The 5-Component Non-Response Policy Engine (NonResponsePolicyEngine): Monitors multi-call unanswered sequences over rolling time horizons. It aggregates Missed-Call Accumulation (0–40 pts), Routine Deviation against learned diurnal baselines (0–25 pts), Persistence of Non-Response across elapsed time (0–15 pts), Multiple Distinct Callers attempting contact (0–10 pts), and Historical Response Deviation (0–10 pts).

[105] To prevent caregiver panic and alert fatigue, SafeCall AI routes all anomalous events through a rigorous 3-Gate Anomaly Pipeline: Gate 1 requires an accumulation of ≥4 consecutive missed calls within 15 minutes; Gate 2 enforces a 60-second grace cancellation timer that automatically aborts if the senior answers a call, dials out, or touches the mobile handset; and Gate 3 validates that the urgency score meets the caregiver-configured threshold (60, 70, or 80 points) within active monitoring schedules.

[106] For interactive presentation and caregiver supervision, SafeCall AI provides two complementary React 18 interfaces: an on-device React 18 single-page application embedded in Android WebView via SafeCallNativeBridge, and a standalone Caregiver Web Dashboard (safecall-dashboard) running on Node.js. The caregiver dashboard provides an interactive visualizer of the 4-factor engine, featuring 10 clinical benchmark scenarios, live urgency gauges, audible siren testing, and real-time alert event logging.

[157] 3.4  SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES APPLIED

[158] 1. Encapsulation:
State encapsulation is strictly enforced in models such as CallContext, CallerProfile, CallRecord, and PendingSequence. All internal fields are protected and accessed via immutable getters or controlled mutators. Database access is strictly encapsulated within CallRepository and PendingAlertRepository, preventing telephony receivers from executing raw SQL queries.

[159] 2. Inheritance:
Extensible base classes reduce redundancy across the system. DatabaseHelper inherits lifecycle management and version migrations from SQLiteOpenHelper. SafeCallPhoneStateReceiver extends BroadcastReceiver. In the presentation tier, React's component hierarchy provides reusable visual abstractions.

[160] 3. Abstraction:
High-level abstractions decouple business rules from infrastructure. The WeightedRuleEngine exposes a single public abstraction evaluateCall(CallContext context), hiding its 4 internal mathematical scoring gates. Similarly, FamilyAlertManager provides dispatchAlert(PendingSequence seq), abstracting the dual-tier routing between cellular SMS and cloud HTTP endpoints.

[161] 4. Polymorphism:
Polymorphism is demonstrated in the handling of call outcomes through the CallOutcome enumeration (ANSWERED, MISSED, DECLINED, OUTGOING_CALLBACK), where each outcome triggers distinct state transitions within CallEventProcessor and SequenceTracker. Furthermore, alert payloads serialize polymorphically for cloud FCM JSON versus cellular SMS string payloads.

[230] 5.2  FUNCTIONAL RESULTS

[231] 5.2.1  Telephony Interception & Call State FSM

[232] The telephony Finite State Machine correctly classified call lifecycle transitions across dozens of physical and simulated calls:

[233] •  IDLE to RINGING: Accurately recorded ringStartTime and incoming caller number within < 5 milliseconds of carrier broadcast arrival.

[234] •  RINGING to IDLE (0s Duration): Correctly classified as MISSED outcome, triggering SequenceTracker accumulation and grace timer initiation.

[235] •  RINGING to OFFHOOK: Correctly identified as ANSWERED call, recording talk duration and instantly cancelling any active grace timers.

[236] •  IDLE to OFFHOOK: Identified as OUTGOING_CALLBACK, indicating that the senior is active and dialing out, resolving any pending emergency sequences.

[237] 5.2.2  The 4-Factor Weighted Urgency Engine

[238] The WeightedRuleEngine was validated against 11 rigorous edge-case unit test scenarios, passing 100% of assertions:

[239] •  Normal Single Call: Score = 0 pts | Level: LOW (0/25 contact + 0/35 freq + 0/10 time + 0/30 history).

[240] •  Trusted Contact Repeated (3 calls): Score = 37 pts | Level: MEDIUM (+15 contact + 22 freq + 0 time + 0 history).

[241] •  Emergency Late-Night Repeated (4 calls): Score = 70 pts | Level: MEDIUM (+25 contact + 35 freq + 10 time + 0 history).

[242] •  Historical Missed Call Anomaly: Score = +30 pts awarded when contact answer rate drops below 50% across ≥3 historical calls.

[243] •  Score Clamping: Guaranteed mathematical bounds [0, 100] even under stacked maximum rule triggers.

[244] 5.2.3  The 5-Component Non-Response Policy Engine & 3-Gate Pipeline

[245] The NonResponsePolicyEngine and 3-Gate Pipeline eliminated false alarms with complete accuracy:

[246] •  Gate 1 (Frequency Filter): Calls with <4 missed events remained in ACCUMULATING status, preventing premature caregiver alerts.

[247] •  Gate 2 (60-Second Grace Timer): Upon reaching 4 missed calls, a 60s countdown was triggered. When the senior touched the app or picked up a callback, the timer aborted immediately with status RESOLVED.

[248] •  Gate 3 (Threshold & Schedule Check): If zero senior activity occurred during the 60s window and the score exceeded the threshold, DISPATCHED status was engaged.

[249] 5.2.4  Embedded SQLite Storage & Offline Resilient Queue

[250] Under simulated network dead zones (airplane mode), emergency alerts were safely serialized to the offline_alerts table. Upon network restoration, CallEventProcessor.drainOfflineAlerts() flushed all queued payloads to the cloud endpoint with zero data loss, while direct carrier SMS was dispatched immediately via cellular infrastructure.

[263] SafeCall AI proposes a context-aware approach for estimating the urgency of incoming communication. The system successfully combines caller identity, call frequency, timing, and historical behaviour through a weighted rule-based engine that produces an urgency score bounded between 0–100.
